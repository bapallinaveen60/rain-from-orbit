<Talk char="dot2">That blue curve is an *assumption* though. What if the assumption is wrong for my particular storm?</Talk>

<Talk char="core">It usually is. So I do not assume one curve. I try **a whole family of them** and keep the one that fits everything else I know.</Talk>

</Box>

<Box tone="d" step={11}>

The family is indexed by a single adjustment factor, **ε**. The Solver runs the entire retrieval repeatedly, sweeping

<Calc>{`ε from [[hl:0.2]] to [[hl:5.0]]`}</Calc>

and picks the value that minimises a cost. In V7, ε is even allowed to **vary with range** rather than being fixed for the whole profile.

</Box>

<Box tone="c" span step={12}>

The cost has four terms. Three for the single-frequency algorithms, all four for dual-frequency:

<Psst>That is the whole retrieval. It is not a formula — it is a **constrained optimisation** run independently for every pixel in every orbit.</Psst>
