<b>Consequence one.</b> `precipRate` carries **different effective uncertainty in different regimes** — tight where the SRT is reliable and the DFR is informative; loose over land, in light rain, where neither is.

A single bulk error bar for a whole product is close to meaningless.

</Box>

<Box tone="d" step={14}>

<b>Consequence two.</b> The `epsilon` field is a **diagnostic**, not just an internal variable.

It tells you how far the algorithm had to move from its climatological DSD prior to reconcile the observations. A systematically non-unity ε over your region is telling you something physical about the microphysics there.

</Box>
