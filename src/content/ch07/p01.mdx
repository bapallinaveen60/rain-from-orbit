<Box tone="d" step={2}>

<b>Three algorithms share this framework:</b> Ku-only, Ka-only, and the dual-frequency DPR algorithm that takes both single-frequency products as its input.

The two single-frequency runs go first, in either order. The dual-frequency run goes last.

<Psst>Each processes one orbit at a time — but a whole month has to be processed as a unit for the databases to come out right.</Psst>

</Box>

<Box tone="c" step={3}>

<b>The ordering is not arbitrary:</b>

- **CSF before DSD** — the drop size assumptions depend on rain type and bright-band position.
- **DSD before SLV** — the Solver needs the scattering tables DSD selected.
- **SRT anywhere** between VER and SLV — it only needs clean surface cross-sections.
