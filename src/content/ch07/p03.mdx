The same single ray is carried the whole way. At each stage, watch what gets **added** to it — not replaced. By the end, every marker on the plot was put there by a different module.

<Note><b>NOTE:</b> the single-frequency chain actually loops **twice**. CSF runs once on the non-precipitation-corrected reflectivity, then again on the Solver's fully corrected output before the convective decision is final. That is why a profile can measure 39 dBZ and still be called convective.</Note>
