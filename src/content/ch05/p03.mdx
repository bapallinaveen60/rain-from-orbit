Four steps. Follow them in order and the result is unavoidable.

<Calc>{`[[b:Step 1.]] Split the distribution into "how many" × "what shape":

        N(D) = [[s:N_{w}]] × n(D ; [[s:D_{m}]])
[[cm:                   ↑ a number      ↑ a curve whose shape depends only on Dm]]`}</Calc>

<Calc>{`[[b:Step 2.]] Reflectivity is an integral over N(D), and [[s:N_{w}]] is just a constant
          multiplier, so it comes straight out of the integral:

        Z([[kut:Ku]]) = [[s:N_{w}]] × I_{Ku}([[s:D_{m}]])
        Z([[kat:Ka]]) = [[s:N_{w}]] × I_{Ka}([[s:D_{m}]])
[[cm:                        ↑ these two differ — that is the whole point]]`}</Calc>

<Calc>{`[[b:Step 3.]] A difference of logs is the log of a ratio:

        DFR = 10 log_{10} Z([[kut:Ku]]) − 10 log_{10} Z([[kat:Ka]]) = 10 log_{10} [ Z([[kut:Ku]]) / Z([[kat:Ka]]) ]`}</Calc>

<Calc>{`[[b:Step 4.]] Substitute Step 2 into Step 3:

                        [[s:N_{w}]] × I_{Ku}([[s:D_{m}]])              I_{Ku}([[s:D_{m}]])
        DFR = 10 log_{10} ───────────────── = 10 log_{10} ──────────
                        [[s:N_{w}]] × I_{Ka}([[s:D_{m}]])              I_{Ka}([[s:D_{m}]])`}</Calc>

The <span className="sv">N<sub>w</sub></span> on the top and the <span className="sv">N<sub>w</sub></span> on the bottom are the same number. They cancel. Exactly, not approximately.
