<Calc>{`v([[o:1]]) = 3.78 × 1^{0.67} = 3.78 m/s    →  D^{3}v =   1 × 3.78 =   [[o:3.78]]

<Calc>{`R = 6π × 10^{−4} × 265.0 = [[hl:0.50 mm h^{−1}]]`}</Calc>

Half a millimetre an hour. Light drizzle.
