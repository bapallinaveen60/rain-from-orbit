<b>Try this.</b> Press **Box A**, then **Box B**. Watch Z sit still at 29.0 dBZ while R jumps about **tenfold** — the two boxes you just computed by hand, now as continuous distributions.

<Note style={{ margin: "0 0 10px" }}><b>NOTE:</b> tenfold here, elevenfold on paper. A gamma distribution with D<sub>m</sub> = 2.72 mm is not literally three drops of 1, 2 and 3 mm — it has a spread of sizes around that mean — so the rain rate comes out slightly different. The *effect* is identical; only the third digit moves.</Note>

<b>Then try this.</b> Set D<sub>m</sub> to 1.20 mm, press **Lock Z**, and drag D<sub>m</sub> up to 2.20 mm. Reflectivity stays pinned; the rain rate falls fourfold.

<Psst>The last readout is the one that should worry you: **a** is the constant in the Z–R relation everyone writes down as if it were a property of nature. Watch it swing by an order of magnitude as you move the sliders.</Psst>
