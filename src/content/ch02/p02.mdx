<Box tone="c" span step={5}>

Two formulas, and that is all the physics we need for now. **Reflectivity** is the sum of the sixth powers of the diameters:

<Calc>{`[[b:Z = Σ D^{6}]]   [mm^{6} m^{−3}] [[cm:— sum over every drop in the box]]`}</Calc>

And **rain rate** is the volume of water passing through, so drop volume times how fast it falls:

<Calc>{`[[b:R = 6π × 10^{−4} × Σ D^{3} v(D)]]   [mm h^{−1}]
[[cm:   where v(D) = 3.78 × D^{0.67} metres per second — bigger drops fall faster]]`}</Calc>

<Note><b>NOTE:</b> Why D⁶? Because for drops much smaller than the wavelength, scattering is **Rayleigh**, and the backscatter cross-section of a sphere goes as the sixth power of its diameter. Chapter 5 is about what happens when that stops being true.</Note>
