<Box step={2}>

<b>The problem:</b> the echo and the rain rate are two *different* summaries of the same population of drops, and they weight that population very differently.

- The **echo** cares about <var style={{ fontFamily: "var(--f-mono)" }}>D⁶</var>.
- The **rain rate** cares about roughly <var style={{ fontFamily: "var(--f-mono)" }}>D³·⁷</var>.

Nearly double the power. That gap is the whole chapter.

</Box>

<Box tone="d" span step={3}>

<b>How we are going to prove it:</b> not with an integral. With **three raindrops in a box**, and arithmetic you can check on paper.

Then we will build a second box that gives Core *exactly the same echo* and rains eleven times harder.

<Psst><b>Psst!</b> Real rain has thousands of drops per cubic metre. We are using three so the numbers stay small enough to follow. Josh would approve.</Psst>
