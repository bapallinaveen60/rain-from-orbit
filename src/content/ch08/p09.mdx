The Level-2 products live at NASA's PPS (`arthurhou.pps.eosdis.nasa.gov`) and JAXA's G-Portal, in HDF5. `2A.GPM.DPR` is the dual-frequency Level-2 file.

Everything this guide referenced is in there: `zFactorMeasured`, `zFactorFinal`, `precipRate`, `paramDSD`, `typePrecip`, `flagBB`, `binClutterFreeBottom`, `pathAtten`, `epsilon`.

</Box>

<Box tone="d" step={26}>

<b>Two habits worth forming:</b>

- Read `flagEcho` and the quality flags **before** the science fields.
- When a number surprises you, walk back up the chain rather than arguing with the end of it.

<Note style={{ marginTop: "10px" }}><b>NOTE:</b> the GPM/DPR Level-2 ATBD is the authority for what each field means. The user manual is the authority for flag bit definitions — which this guide has deliberately not reproduced, because they change between versions.</Note>
