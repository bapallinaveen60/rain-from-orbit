<Header>Six Things That Will Catch You: Details Part 5</Header>

<Grid>

<Box tone="a" step={17}>

<b>1. The range axis runs top-down.</b> Array index 0 is about 21–22 km up; index 175 is at the ellipsoid. Height *decreases* as the index increases, which is the opposite of every intuition.

</Box>

<Box step={18}>

<b>2. The fill value is −9999.9.</b> It is a large negative number, not a NaN. Average without masking it and your rain rates come out catastrophically negative.

<Calc code>{`rate = np.where(rate < [[o:-1000]], np.nan, rate)`}</Calc>

</Box>

<Box tone="d" step={19}>

<b>3. `typePrecip` is eight digits.</b> Testing `== 2` matches nothing. The leading digit is the category:

<Calc code>{`major = ptype // [[o:10000000]]   [[cm:# 1 strat, 2 conv, 3 other]]`}</Calc>

</Box>

<Box tone="c" step={20}>

<b>4. `binClutterFreeBottom` is a bin number, not a height — and bin numbers start at 1.</b> The same goes for `binRealSurface`, `binStormTop` and the other `bin…` fields. Subtract one before indexing the range axis, or every height comes out a bin (125 m) too low:

<Calc code>{`h_cfb = hgt[s, r, cfb[s, r\\] - [[o:1]]\\]   [[cm:# not hgt[s, r, cfb[s, r\\]\\]]]`}</Calc>

Treat everything below it as extrapolated — Chapter 3.

</Box>

<Box tone="a" step={21}>

<b>5. The May 2018 scan change.</b> Before it, `Ka/FS` has no data in angle bins 1–12 and 38–49. Any statistic you compute across the full swath will silently mix two different instruments unless you split on that date.

</Box>

<Box tone="d" step={22}>

<b>6. `zFactorCorrected` became `zFactorFinal` in V07.</b> Every tutorial written before about 2022 uses the old name.

<Psst>Five of these six are version or geometry traps, not science. That is the usual ratio.</Psst>

</Box>
