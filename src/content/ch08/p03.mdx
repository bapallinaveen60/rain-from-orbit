<b>Press "Match Z".</b> Both spells are set to the same reflectivity — the same thing a single-frequency radar would measure — and the true rain rates still differ by a factor of more than two.

At any fixed reflectivity, dry-spell microphysics implies about **2.3 times** the rain rate of wet-spell microphysics — and that factor barely moves with rain rate, because at fixed D<sub>m</sub> both Z and R scale with concentration. No single <code>Z = aR<sup>b</sup></code> can sit on both.

<Psst>This is Chapter 2's ambiguity, in published numbers, over the terrain you care about.</Psst>
