PIA reliability drops exactly where attenuation correction matters most — and cost term <b>(b)</b> from Chapter 7 weakens with it.

<Alert title="AND THE PRIOR FIGHTS YOU!!!">

Cost term <b>(a)</b> anchors ε to a prior from the DSD database. If that prior reflects a global or tropical-mean microphysics, and the Western Ghats in a break spell is running a bimodal, small-drop distribution, then the algorithm is being pulled toward the wrong answer **by its own regularisation**.

<p style={{ marginBottom: 0 }}>The retrieved `epsilon` field over the region, averaged by monsoon phase, is a direct measurement of that pull. It is the first thing worth plotting.</p>
