Here is the step that closes the argument — the **N/N model**, for *non-breakup, non-coalescence*. If no particle splits or merges on the way down, then the number crossing each level per second must be the **same at every level**. Speed up, and the concentration has to fall:

<Calc>{`N(D_{b}) ∝ V_{R}(D_{melt}) / V_{b}(D_{b})
[[cm:   go three times faster, and there are a third as many of you per cubic metre]]`}</Calc>

<Talk char="dot">So by the time I am a raindrop, I am **a third the diameter** and there are **three times fewer** of me in each cubic metre than there were snowflakes.</Talk>

<Talk char="core2">Which is precisely why my echo collapses below the bright band. Not because the rain stopped — because the targets got small and sparse at the same time.</Talk>
