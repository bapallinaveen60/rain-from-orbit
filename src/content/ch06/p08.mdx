Those are not simulations. Panels (a) and (b) are **stratiform**, (c) and (d) **convective**, measured from an airborne precipitation radar — and every claim on the previous page is visible in them.

- In (a) the bright band is the obvious bump near 4 km, and below it **DFR<sub>m</sub> in (b) is flat and small** — barely 5 dB, barely changing with height.
- In (c) the convective profile has no such bump, and **DFR<sub>m</sub> in (d) climbs steadily all the way down**.

<Psst>The marked <b>DFR<sub>m</sub>(max)</b> and <b>DFR<sub>m</sub>(min)</b> are the quantities the classifier actually measures: their ratio becomes the index *V1*, and the mean slope below the minimum becomes *V2*.</Psst>
