Chapter 2 treated every particle as a sphere of water. The algorithm does not. It models each one as a sphere of **three things at once**:

<Calc>{`P_{w} + P_{i} + P_{a} = 1
[[cm:   liquid water + solid ice + air, by volume]]`}</Calc>

and tracks its bulk density <b>ρ<sub>b</sub></b> in g/cm³ as the mixture changes.

For a **mixed-phase** particle the ATBD sets the water fraction from a melting model, then gets the density from a startlingly simple empirical rule:

<Calc>{`[[b:ρ_{b} = √P_{w}]]`}</Calc>

That square root is doing a lot of work. Run it:
