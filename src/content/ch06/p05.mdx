Now follow **one particle** whose melted mass would make a 1 mm raindrop, all the way down. Two things change together.

First its **size**. A low-density particle holding a fixed mass must be bigger:

<Calc>{`D_{b} = D_{melt} · ρ_{b}^{−1/3}`}</Calc>

Then its **fall speed**, which the ATBD gives in three density regimes:

<Calc>{`V_{b} = 3.3 √(ρ_{b} − ρ_{a})                    ρ_{b} ≤ 0.05
V_{b} = 8.8 √(0.1 D_{b}(ρ_{b} − ρ_{a}))          0.05 < ρ_{b} ≤ 0.30
V_{b} = interpolate to the raindrop speed      0.30 < ρ_{b} ≤ 1.00`}</Calc>

</Box>
