<Alert title="TERMINOLOGY ALERT!!!">

`typePrecip` catches people out constantly. It is not 1, 2, 3. The **leading digits** give the major category; the **trailing digits** record which method decided it and on what evidence.

<p style={{ marginBottom: 0 }}>If you write `typePrecip == 2` you will match nothing at all. Divide down to the leading digit, or consult the user manual's bit definitions.</p>
