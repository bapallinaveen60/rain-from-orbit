<Box step={8}>

A **radiometer** measures a brightness temperature — one number, summed through the entire column of atmosphere beneath it.

<Talk char="core2">I can see *that* there is water down there. I cannot see whether it is at 2 km or at 10 km, whether it is frozen or melted, or where it changes from one to the other.</Talk>

</Box>

<Box tone="a" step={9}>

A **radar** transmits a pulse and times the echo. And timing is the whole trick:

<Calc>{`every echo arrives with its own [[hl:range]] attached
[[cm:…and range, from a satellite looking down, is height.]]`}</Calc>

So a radar does not return a picture of a storm. It returns a **vertical slice through it**, kilometre after kilometre along the orbit track.

</Box>
