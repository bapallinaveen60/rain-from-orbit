So one radar is not the global product. It is the **ruler that calibrates one**.

Because GMI and DPR fly on the same platform, every overpass gives a matched pair: what a radiometer sees, and what the radar underneath it knows is actually there.

</Box>

<Box tone="a" step={16}>

Those pairs train the retrieval used on every other radiometer in the **GPM constellation** — DMSP's SSMIS, GCOM-W1's AMSR-2, MetOp's MHS, and the ATMS instruments on NOAA-20, NOAA-21 and Suomi-NPP.

<Psst>None of those carries a radar. Every one of them borrows Core's.</Psst>

</Box>

<Box tone="d" span step={17}>

The merged product is **IMERG** — Integrated Multi-satellitE Retrievals for GPM.
