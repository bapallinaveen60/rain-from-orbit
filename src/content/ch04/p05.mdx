The classical fix. Assume a power law between attenuation and reflectivity…

<Calc>{`k = [[s:α]] Z_{e}^{[[s:β]]}`}</Calc>

…substitute it into the integral equation, and it solves in closed form:

<Calc>{`Z_{e}(r) = [[ov:Z_{m}]](r) ÷ [ 1 − 0.2 ln(10) [[s:β]] ∫_{0}^{r} [[s:α]] [[ov:Z_{m}]]^{[[s:β]]} ds ]^{1/[[s:β]]}`}</Calc>

</Box>

<Box tone="c" step={13}>

Now look only at the **denominator**. As the integrated attenuation grows, the bracket shrinks toward zero — and the correction goes to infinity.

<Talk char="core2">So the formula works beautifully in light rain, where I did not need it, and detonates in heavy rain, where I did.</Talk>
