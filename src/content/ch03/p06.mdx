<Box tone="a" step={14}>

An HDF5 file is a tree. The top branches of a DPR Level-2 file are **swaths**, and they were reorganised in Version 7.

- **V06** had three: `NS` (normal, Ku full swath), `MS` (matched, inner 25), `HS` (high sensitivity).
- **V07** has two: `FS` (full swath, dual frequency) and `HS`.

<Calc>{`FS: [[hl:49]] angle bins × [[hl:176]] range bins, 125 m apart`}</Calc>

</Box>

<Box tone="c" span step={15}>

<Alert title="THE TRAP IN THIS FIGURE!!!">

V07 applies the `FS` format to data from *both sides* of the 2018 scan change — but the data behind it is not the same.

<p style={{ marginBottom: 0 }}><b>Before 21 May 2018:</b> `Ka/FS` is missing angle bins **1–12 and 38–49**. <b>After:</b> `Ka/HS` is missing angle bins **1–24**. Same variable names, same shapes, silently different coverage.</p>

</Alert>
