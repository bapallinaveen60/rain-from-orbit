<Box tone="a" step={4}>

That is the real thing, from the ATBD. Three different sets of footprints, and they are not all the same radar.

- **Pale blue** — <span className="kut">KuPR</span>, 49 beams, the full swath.
- **Yellow** — <span className="kat">KaPR</span> **matched beams**, pointed at the same spots as the central 25 Ku beams so the two bands see the same volume of air.
- **Pink** — <span className="kat">KaPR</span> **high-sensitivity beams**, interlaced *between* the matched ones, with a longer pulse.

</Box>

<Box tone="d" step={5} style={{ marginTop: "13px" }}>

<Talk char="dot2">Why two Ka scan patterns? Pick one.</Talk>

<Talk char="core">Because matching Ku exactly is what makes DFR possible — but a longer pulse on its own beams detects lighter snow. I could not have both in one beam, so I alternated.</Talk>

</Box>

</div>

<Box tone="c" span step={6}>

On **21 May 2018** the KaPR scan pattern was changed so that Ka covers the **full swath**, not just the inner 25 beams. Dual-frequency retrieval became available edge to edge.

<Psst>Remember that date. It is the single most important thing to know before you open a file, and page 7 of this chapter explains why.</Psst>
