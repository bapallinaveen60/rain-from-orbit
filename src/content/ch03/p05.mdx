<Alert title="A TRAP!!!">

Every echo above the noise floor is converted to reflectivity and written out — *including* echoes below the precipitation detection threshold.

<p style={{ marginBottom: 0 }}>`zFactorMeasured` having a value does **not** mean the algorithm considered it rain. `flagEcho` is what tells you that. Read the flags before you read the science fields.</p>
